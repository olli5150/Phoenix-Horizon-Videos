# 🎬 PHOENIX HORIZON — “ZOMBIE NATION”
## Cinematic Music Video Story Treatment
### Working Title:
# THEY KEEP COMING BACK

---

# 🎭 CORE THEME

The video is not about literal zombies.

It is about:

- humanity repeating old mistakes
- society falling back into fear and manipulation
- emotional numbness
- ideological extremism
- conspiracy thinking
- climate collapse
- mass manipulation through media
- collective memory loss

The “living dead” are people who stopped thinking and feeling for themselves.

The horror is subtle and psychological.

---

# 🎨 VISUAL STYLE

- cinematic dystopian realism
- slow-burn tension
- rain & storm atmosphere
- cold neon reflections
- old television flicker
- practical lighting
- analog textures
- emotionally grounded acting
- no over-the-top horror

Inspired by:

- Night of the Living Dead
- Children of Men
- Dark
- Midnight Mass
- The Road
- Lost Forever

---

# 🏚️ MAIN LOCATION

An isolated old countryside house outside a collapsing city.

The house becomes:

- refuge
- prison
- metaphor for society

Outside:
a growing crowd of silent people gathers in the rain.

---

# 🎬 SONG STRUCTURE → VIDEO STRUCTURE

---

# INTRO
## “THE STATIC”

### VISUALS

Black screen.

Old analog TV static.

Emergency broadcasts flicker.

Quick fragmented images:

- riots
- floods
- forest fires
- collapsing glaciers
- conspiracy livestreams
- political speeches
- screaming news anchors
- social media rage posts

Audio is distorted.

Rain begins.

---

### MAIN CHARACTER INTRODUCTION

A young woman drives alone through heavy rain.

The radio constantly changes between:

- political propaganda
- fear-driven news
- conspiracy theories
- religious extremists
- advertisements

She turns the radio off.

Silence.

Lightning reveals:
people standing motionless on roadsides.

---

# VERSE 1
## “THE HOUSE”

The woman reaches an isolated old house.

Inside are strangers:

- an older history teacher
- a father with his daughter
- a paranoid conspiracy theorist
- a mechanic
- a nurse

Nobody trusts each other.

Electricity flickers.

TV screens continue broadcasting chaos.

---

### SUBTLE DETAILS

Background television:

- debates about “national restoration”
- anti-science propaganda
- migration panic
- climate denial
- extremist rallies

Nothing is named directly.

Everything feels familiar.

---

# PRE-CHORUS
## “THEY ARRIVE”

Someone notices figures outside.

People stand silently in the rain.

Motionless.

Watching the house.

More appear slowly.

No screaming.
No attacks.

Just staring.

The atmosphere becomes deeply unsettling.

---

### FIRST BAND APPEARANCE

Quick cinematic cuts:

- Leo singing alone in a dark radio station
- Luke playing guitar under flickering streetlights
- Gloria tuning a bass beside old analog equipment
- Pete restarting industrial generators

The band feels ghostlike.
Almost mythical.

---

# CHORUS 1
## “ZOMBIE NATION”

The crowd outside grows larger.

The people outside begin repeating phrases:

- political slogans
- conspiracy fragments
- media talking points

Like hypnotized echoes.

Inside the house,
arguments erupt.

Everyone blames someone else.

Nobody listens.

---

### VISUAL SYMBOLISM

Crosscut between:

- old war archive footage
- modern protests
- social media doomscrolling
- burning forests
- manipulated videos
- nationalist imagery
- empty supermarket shelves

History loops endlessly.

---

# VERSE 2
## “THE INFECTION”

Paranoia spreads inside the house.

The conspiracy theorist convinces others:

- the broadcasts are lies
- the outsiders are enemies
- someone inside caused all of this

Trust collapses.

The father becomes aggressive.

The teacher tries explaining:

> “Every generation thinks it’s different.”

Nobody listens.

---

### OUTSIDE

The silent crowd grows impossible in size.

Thousands.

Standing still in rain and fog.

Like a dead civilization waiting.

---

# PRE-CHORUS 2
## “THE SIGNAL”

Power suddenly cuts out.

Darkness.

Then:
a hidden radio frequency activates.

Phoenix Horizon broadcasts live.

Leo sings directly into camera.

Not like a rockstar.

Like someone trying to warn humanity one last time.

---

### EMOTIONAL SHIFT

The young woman listens.

For the first time:
someone truly hears.

The music cuts through the fear.

---

# GUITAR SOLO
## “MEMORY”

Luke’s solo plays over dreamlike imagery:

People outside briefly “wake up.”

Tiny moments:

- a man drops a propaganda flag
- someone hugs their child
- a woman removes headphones
- someone looks at the sky instead of a screen

The crowd flickers between:
humanity and emptiness.

---

### VISUAL STYLE

Slow motion.
Rain.
Neon reflections.
Dust particles.
Archive overlays.
Ghostlike memories of happier times.

---

# BRIDGE
## “THE COLLAPSE”

Inside the house:
complete breakdown.

Fear wins.

The group turns against itself.

Violence erupts.

The house catches fire accidentally.

Outside:
the silent crowd watches emotionlessly.

No reaction.

---

### PETE SCENE

Pete desperately keeps generators alive while sparks fly around him.

Like:
the last machine still running in a dying civilization.

---

# FINAL CHORUS
## “THEY KEEP COMING BACK”

The burning house illuminates the endless crowd.

The young woman escapes outside.

She walks through the silent people.

Nobody stops her.

She realizes:

They are not monsters.

They are ordinary people who surrendered themselves.

---

### FINAL BAND PERFORMANCE

Phoenix Horizon performs on the rooftop of the burning building during the final chorus.

Massive cinematic atmosphere:

- storm
- wind
- rain
- firelight
- neon glow
- smoke
- emotional close-ups

Not triumph.

More like:
a final warning.

---

# OUTRO
## “THE CHILD”

Morning.

The storm is gone.

Ash falls like snow.

The crowd has disappeared.

Only one small child remains standing in the empty road.

The child finds:
an old portable radio.

Phoenix Horizon still plays faintly.

The child looks toward the horizon.

---

# FINAL LINE (OPTIONAL)

A distorted radio voice says softly:

> “Why does it always return?”

Cut to black.

---

# 🎥 FINAL MESSAGE

The video never says:
“this ideology is evil.”

Instead it says:

> humanity keeps resurrecting its own nightmares.

That makes the story:

- timeless
- emotional
- intelligent
- cinematic
- disturbing without being preachy

And perfectly aligned with Phoenix Horizon’s identity.
