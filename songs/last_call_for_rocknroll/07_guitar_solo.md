\# SCENE FLOW



As the chorus energy fades,

Luke exits the LAST CALL club alone.



The city lights glow behind him.



Outside:



The wasteland stretches endlessly.



Luke climbs onto the roof of the rusty tour bus.



Wind moves through his hair and clothes.



The guitar solo begins.



The emotional tone becomes dreamlike.



Sand drifts through neon light.



The camera moves around Luke in slow cinematic motion.



At times:



The world around him almost disappears into darkness.



Only:



\* the guitar

\* the neon light

\* the music

&#x20; remain.



Meanwhile:



Across the wasteland,

people continue hearing the signal.



Some stand silently under the night sky listening.



Others drive through empty highways following the music.



The solo becomes:



> a signal of emotional freedom.



The scene slowly builds toward emotional exhaustion.



The world cannot hold this feeling forever.



Darkness begins returning.
