\# CHARACTER OVERVIEW



\## ROLE IN THE FILM



Leo is:



> the emotional voice of the dying world.



He is not portrayed as:



\* a superstar

\* a classic rock frontman

\* an action hero



Instead:



Leo feels like:



\* the last storyteller

\* a tired poet

\* a wandering survivor

\* someone carrying memories for humanity



His presence should always feel:



\* human

\* vulnerable

\* emotionally honest

\* cinematic

\* melancholic



\---



\# CORE PERSONALITY



\## CHARACTER ENERGY



\* emotionally observant

\* reflective

\* calm

\* emotionally exhausted

\* compassionate

\* deeply sincere



Leo rarely speaks outside the lyrics.



Most emotion is communicated through:



\* facial expression

\* silence

\* eye movement

\* breathing

\* subtle body language



\---



\# EMOTIONAL FUNCTION



Leo represents:



\* memory

\* emotional survival

\* hope through music

\* human vulnerability

\* emotional truth



When he sings,

it should feel like:



> he is speaking directly

> to people who forgot how to feel.
