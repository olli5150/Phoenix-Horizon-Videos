\# UNDERGROUND BUNKER

\## Phoenix Horizon – Cinematic Environment \& Survival Sanctuary



\---



\# 🕳️ CORE VIBE



A hidden underground refuge beneath the ruins of the world.



The Underground Bunker is:

\- shelter

\- rehearsal room

\- war room

\- emotional prison

\- last surviving sanctuary for music



This is where Phoenix Horizon survives

after the collapse.



The bunker represents:

\- survival through music

\- isolation

\- emotional pressure

\- human connection in darkness

\- the last signal of humanity



The atmosphere should feel:

claustrophobic,

warm,

industrial,

emotionally intimate.



\---



\# 🌑 GENERAL ATMOSPHERE



The bunker feels:

\- old

\- improvised

\- functional

\- lived in

\- emotionally exhausted



This is NOT:

a sci-fi military base.



Instead:

\- old concrete walls

\- exposed pipes

\- leaking ceilings

\- industrial lights

\- analog equipment

\- cables everywhere

\- generators humming constantly



The bunker survives through:

maintenance,

effort,

and stubborn hope.



\---
