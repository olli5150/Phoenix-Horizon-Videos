\# ABANDONED DINER

\## Phoenix Horizon – Cinematic Visual Scene



\---



\## 🎬 CORE VIBE



A lonely roadside diner somewhere between memory and collapse.



The place feels frozen in time:

flickering neon signs,

dust in the air,

old chrome reflections,

empty coffee cups,

late-night silence.



The diner represents:

\- lost youth

\- emotional exhaustion

\- broken dreams

\- the last stop before dawn



Visual tone:

melancholic, cinematic, emotional, timeless.



\---



\# 🌆 ENVIRONMENT



\## Exterior



\- abandoned roadside diner in the middle of nowhere

\- retro americana aesthetic

\- flickering neon sign

\- cracked asphalt parking lot

\- old motorcycles parked outside

\- rain puddles reflecting neon light

\- desert highway atmosphere

\- slight fog or dust in the air



Color palette:

\- cyan neon

\---



\# 👤 LEO IN THE DINER



Leo usually:

\- sits alone in a booth

\- looks through rain-covered windows

\- wears black leather jacket

\- fingers wrapped around coffee mug

\- emotionally distant

\- cigarette smoke drifting through frame



Camera:

\- medium close-ups

\- reflections in glass

\- shallow depth of field



Emotion:

trying not to fall apart.



Reference consistency based on official Leo identity:

\- dark shoulder-length hair

\- light stubble

\- intense melancholic eyes

\- rugged cinematic realism

\- never glam-rock styling



\---



\# 👤 GLORIA IN THE DINER



Gloria usually:

\- leans silently against the counter

\- sits across from Leo without speaking

\- calm controlled posture

\- black leather outfit

\- subtle smoky eyes

\- platinum blonde hair softly illuminated by neon



Emotion:

quiet understanding without words.



Visual energy:

stillness inside emotional chaos.



\---



\# 👤 LUKE IN THE DINER



Luke appears:

\- exhausted after a show

\- blue neon reflecting on guitar case



Emotion:

nostalgia mixed with emotional fatigue.
