\# SCENE FLOW



The scene opens with the band stopping at an abandoned gas station.



Weak neon lights flicker in the darkness.



Luke exits the bus and looks across the empty wasteland.



The atmosphere feels frozen in time.



Leo enters an abandoned diner.



Dust floats through weak red neon light.



He sits alone in a booth.



The first verse begins.



For the first time,

Leo sings directly to the audience.



Not like a performance.



More like:



\* a memory

\* a confession

\* someone speaking to ghosts



Meanwhile:



Gloria repairs an old tube amplifier inside an underground bunker.



Generators flicker.



Her calm movements contrast the dying world around her.



Elsewhere:



A child discovers old rock cassette tapes.



For the first time in years,

music enters another human life.



At the same time,

small radio signals begin reaching isolated survivors across the wasteland.



The world slowly begins waking up.
