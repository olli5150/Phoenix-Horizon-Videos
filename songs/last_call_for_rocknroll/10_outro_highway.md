\# SCENE FLOW



The final chorus slowly fades.



Inside the LAST CALL club:



The crowd stands silently.



Some people hug.



Others simply sit quietly,

trying to hold onto the feeling.



The stage lights slowly dim.



Leo quietly places the microphone down.



Gloria powers down her amplifier.



Pete shuts off the final generator.



Luke takes one final look at the empty venue.



Nobody speaks.



Outside:



Dawn slowly appears across the wasteland.



The rusty tour bus begins moving again.



Survivors stand outside the club watching silently.



Some wave.



Others simply listen to the fading music.



The bus disappears slowly into the endless highway.



The camera remains behind.



The LAST CALL sign flickers weakly in the distance.



Then:



The old analog radio from the beginning returns.



Static.



A weak voice says:



> “...this was Phoenix Horizon...”



The signal fades.



Black screen.
