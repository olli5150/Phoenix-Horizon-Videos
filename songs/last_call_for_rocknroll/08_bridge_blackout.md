3:20 – 3:45



\## Musical Function



The bridge becomes the emotional collapse of the film.



After the emotional freedom of the solo,

reality suddenly returns.



The electricity begins failing.



The club slowly falls into darkness.



This scene must feel:



\* vulnerable

\* intimate

\* human

\* fragile

\* emotionally raw



The music strips away spectacle.



Only emotion remains.

\# SCENE FLOW



The solo fades.



Inside the LAST CALL club,

the lights begin flickering violently.



The music becomes unstable.



Generators fail one after another.



Pete desperately tries keeping the power alive.



Sparks explode.



Then:



Darkness.



The club falls silent.



Only emergency lights remain.



For a moment,

everyone fears the magic is over.



Then:



Someone in the crowd quietly sings the chorus.



Another voice joins.



Then another.



Soon:



The entire crowd sings together in darkness.



No instruments.



No electricity.



Only human voices.



Leo stands silently,

emotionally overwhelmed.



Gloria closes her eyes.



Luke lowers his guitar.



Pete stops fighting the machinery.



For the first time:



The music no longer belongs to the band.



It belongs completely to the people.



The scene becomes deeply emotional and almost spiritual.
