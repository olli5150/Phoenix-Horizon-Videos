\# COLOR GRADING

\## Phoenix Horizon – Cinematic Color Language \& Emotional Tone



\---



\# 🎨 CORE PHILOSOPHY



Color in Phoenix Horizon videos is NEVER decorative.



Color is:

\- emotion

\- memory

\- atmosphere

\- psychological storytelling



The grading should feel:

cinematic,

weathered,

human,

emotional.



Every frame should look like:

a faded memory from a beautiful collapse.



\---



\# 🌌 OVERALL COLOR IDENTITY



\## Core Palette



Primary colors:

\- cyan neon

\- tungsten amber

\- faded magenta

\- deep blacks

\- dusty orange

\- steel blue

\- dirty chrome silver



Secondary accents:

\- emergency red

\- sodium vapor yellow

\- pale moonlight blue



The world should always feel:

slightly cold,

slightly nostalgic,

and emotionally exhausted.



\---



\# 🎬 OVERALL LOOK



\## General Image Feel



\- cinematic contrast

\- soft halation

\- subtle bloom

\- realistic skin tones

\- lifted shadows

\- deep blacks without crushing detail

\- soft highlight rolloff

\- analog-inspired texture



The image should NEVER feel:

digitally sharp.



\---



\# 🎞️ FILM EMULATION



\## Preferred Texture



The grading should emulate:

\- Kodak Vision3

\- Fuji Eterna

\- 35mm anamorphic cinema

\- slightly aged film stock



Visual texture:

\- subtle grain

\- soft contrast transitions

\- glowing highlights

\- imperfect color separation



The image should feel:

organic and imperfect.



\---



\# 🌑 SHADOW PHILOSOPHY



Shadows are extremely important.



Phoenix Horizon exists:

inside shadows.



Shadows should:

\- retain detail

\- feel soft but deep

\- contain atmosphere

\- carry emotional weight



Never:

pure digital black crush.



\---



\# 💡 HIGHLIGHT PHILOSOPHY



Highlights should glow —

not clip harshly.



Preferred highlight behavior:

\- warm bloom

\- soft practical glow

\- neon halation

\- cinematic rolloff



Important surfaces:

\- chrome reflections

\- wet asphalt

\- leather jackets

\- cigarette smoke

\- skin highlights

\- rain reflections



\---



\# 🌫️ ATMOSPHERIC COLOR INTERACTION



Atmosphere is critical.



The grading must react beautifully with:

\- smoke

\- fog

\- haze

\- dust

\- rain

\- steam



Light beams should:

softly spread through atmosphere.



The air itself should feel colored.

\# 🎥 PERFORMANCE SCENE GRADING



Performance scenes should feel:

\- sweaty

\- smoky

\- emotional

\- explosive



Important visual elements:

\- light beams through smoke

\- warm skin highlights

\- practical stage lights

\- saturated but controlled neon



Never:

EDM festival saturation.



\---



\# 🌧️ RAIN \& WET SURFACES



Rain reflections are a signature element.



Wet surfaces should:

\- softly mirror neon

\- glow under headlights

\- create emotional texture



Especially important for:

\- Neon Highway

\- Last Call Club exteriors

\- motel scenes



\---



\# 🎞️ GRAIN STRUCTURE



\## Grain Philosophy



Grain should:

\- feel cinematic

\- be visible in shadows

\- soften digital perfection

\- create emotional texture



Never:

overly clean digital image.



Preferred:

35mm-style fine grain.



\---



\# ✨ HALATION \& BLOOM



Very important.



Bright lights should:

\- softly bloom

\- glow into darkness

\- interact with atmosphere



Most important around:

\- neon signs

\- headlights

\- practical bulbs

\- reflections

\- stage lights



The image should feel:

alive.



\---



\# 🌈 SATURATION RULES



\## Saturation Philosophy



Colors should NEVER feel:

hyper-modern or overprocessed.



Preferred saturation:

\- slightly muted

\- emotionally controlled

\- natural but cinematic



Only certain lights may become vibrant:

\- neon signs

\- stage lights

\- sunsets

\- emergency lighting



Everything else stays:

grounded and realistic.
