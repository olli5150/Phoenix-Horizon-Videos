\# SHOTLIST

\## Phoenix Horizon – Cinematic Shot Language \& Visual Coverage



\---



\# 🎬 CORE PHILOSOPHY



The shotlist is NOT:

a technical checklist.



It is:

an emotional visual language system.



Every shot in Phoenix Horizon videos must:

\- support emotion

\- deepen atmosphere

\- reinforce character identity

\- build cinematic immersion



The audience should feel:

inside the world.



\---



\# 🎥 SHOT HIERARCHY



Phoenix Horizon visuals are built from:



1\. Emotional Character Shots

2\. Atmospheric Environment Shots

3\. Performance Shots

4\. Symbolic Insert Shots

5\. Transitional Motion Shots



All five categories are equally important.
