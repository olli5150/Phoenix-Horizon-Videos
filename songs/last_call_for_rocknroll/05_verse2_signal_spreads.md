\# SCENE FLOW



The second verse begins with emotional silence after the first chorus.



The LAST CALL club continues glowing in the distance.



Outside the club:



The city slowly changes.



In abandoned apartments,

people sit around old radios listening together.



Some smile for the first time in years.



Some quietly cry.



Old Phoenix Horizon posters become visible on ruined walls.



The world remembers.



Leo walks slowly through empty neon streets while singing.



This lipsync feels:



\* reflective

\* personal

\* emotionally tired



Not performative.



Meanwhile:



Gloria watches the crowd quietly from backstage.



For the first time,

she sees hope returning to people.



The emotional scale of the world expands.



Music is no longer trapped inside the club.



It is becoming:



> a signal spreading through the wasteland.
