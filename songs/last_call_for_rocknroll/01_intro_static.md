\# SCENE FLOW



The screen begins completely black.



Only radio static is heard.



Slowly an old analog radio appears.



Its signal is unstable.



Between the static:



> “...last call for rock’n’roll…”



Then:



The first atmospheric elements of the song begin.



A rusty tour bus drives through an abandoned desert highway at night.



Dust moves across the asphalt.



Far away:

weak neon lights flicker through haze.



Inside the bus:



\* Leo listens to the radio

\* Gloria sleeps with her bass

\* Luke watches the horizon

\* Pete sits silently in darkness



Nobody speaks.



The atmosphere tells the story.

\# WORLD BIBLE



\## THE WORLD



The apocalypse did not happen overnight.



The world slowly became silent.



Music disappeared.

Clubs closed.

Radio stations died.

People stopped dreaming.



Cities still exist,

but they feel emotionally abandoned.



The world is filled with:



\* flickering neon signs

\* empty highways

\* abandoned diners

\* rusted vehicles

\* broken arcades

\* forgotten concert venues

\* dusty motels

\* dying electrical systems



Technology still exists,

but barely survives.



Electricity is unstable.

Generators are precious.

Old radios are emotional relics.



Music feels almost forbidden.



When people hear songs again,

it awakens something human inside them.
