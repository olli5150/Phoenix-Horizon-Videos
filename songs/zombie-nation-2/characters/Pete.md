\# CHARACTER OVERVIEW



\## ROLE IN THE FILM



Pete is:



> the physical heartbeat of the wasteland.



While:



\* Leo represents emotional memory

\* Gloria represents emotional warmth

\* Luke represents emotional freedom



Pete represents:



\* survival

\* resilience

\* momentum

\* energy

\* endurance



Pete is not portrayed as:



\* a flashy drummer

\* a wild performer

\* an extroverted rock cliché



Instead:



He feels like:



\* an old machine still running

\* a survivor keeping the lights alive

\* the engine beneath the emotion



The audience should feel:



> when Pete stops,

> the world stops.
